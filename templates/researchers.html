Meet the Team Behind ItikCare

A capstone research project by BS Information Technology students at Central Bicol State University of Agriculture, Sipocot Campus, built to give small-scale itik farmers a practical, data-driven way to forecast egg yield.

The Researchers

The two developers who designed, built, and validated ItikCare as their thesis project.

Lead Developer & Researcher

Led the Django backend and the Random Forest forecasting pipeline — data preprocessing, model training, and rolling retraining. BS Information Technology, Central Bicol State University of Agriculture, Sipocot Campus.
