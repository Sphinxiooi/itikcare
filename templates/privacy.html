Privacy Notice

ItikCare respects your privacy. This notice explains what personal information we collect when you use ItikCare, why we collect it, and how we protect it, in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

Last updated: September 2026

1. Who we are

ItikCare is a capstone research project of BS Information Technology students at Central Bicol State University of Agriculture, Sipocot Campus. It forecasts daily egg yield and gives farm recommendations for small-scale itik (duck) farms.

2. What we collect

  • Account details: your full name, and your email address and/or mobile number if you give them.
  • Farm location: your barangay in Libmanan, and the approximate map location we look up from it.
  • Farm records: your flock details and the daily data you log (egg count, feed, flock size, temperature, humidity, and similar).
  • Profile picture: only if you upload one.
  • Google sign-in: if you sign in with Google, your Google email and account ID. We never see your Google password.

3. Why we collect it

  • To create your account and let you log in with your name, email, or mobile number.
  • To forecast your egg yield and give recommendations for your farm.
  • To fill in local weather for your daily log, based on your barangay.
  • To send you a code by email if you forget your password, and daily log reminders.
  • For the research study itself: farm records may be analyzed to measure how accurate the forecasts are. Results in the thesis are reported without your name or contact details.

We do not sell your information, share it with advertisers, or send you SMS messages.

4. How we store and protect it

Your information is stored in a password-protected database on our hosting provider. Passwords and reset codes are stored scrambled (hashed), never as plain text. Only you and the system administrators can see your account details.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your account details while your account is active. When you delete your account, we erase your name, email, mobile number, farm location, and profile picture. Your farm records are kept without your personal details, because they help train the forecasting model for other farmers.

6. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act, you have the right to:

  • Be informed about how your data is used (this notice).
  • Access and correct your details at any time from Account Settings.
  • Erase your details by deleting your account from Account Settings.
  • Object or withdraw consent, or file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.

7. Contact us

For questions about your data, contact the researchers: Mark Justine C. Labay and Jhanmae Angela S. Peñaredondo, Central Bicol State University of Agriculture, Sipocot Campus.
